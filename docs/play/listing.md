## Full description (max 4000)

Tinypod is a small, personal podcast app. Subscribe to RSS feeds, sort shows into folders, keep a queue of what you mean to finish, download for offline listening, and pick up exactly where you left off, on the phone or in the car.

No accounts, no server, no ads, no analytics. Everything lives on your phone, and the app only talks to the podcasts' own feeds (plus Apple's public podcast directory when you search).

WHAT IT DOES
• Subscribe by searching for a podcast by name, or paste any RSS feed URL. Feeds refresh when you open the app and every four hours in the background.
• New episodes: one list of everything newer than the last episode you finished, across all your shows, with badges on each podcast and folder.
• Folders: your library is a grid of artwork tiles; folders show a mosaic of the shows inside.
• Player with back 10 s, forward 30 s and 1×–2× speed. The player and podcast pages take their colours from the artwork, in light and dark mode.
• Resume: your position is saved as you listen. After a restart, or when Bluetooth or the car says "play", the last episode carries on where it stopped.
• Queue: episodes stay queued until you finish them. Reorder by dragging.
• History, grouped by day.
• Downloads for offline listening, with a storage overview.
• Episode pages with show notes, working links and chapter timestamps you can tap to jump to.
• Search within a podcast's episodes.
• Works on foldables, tablets and in landscape.

ANDROID AUTO
• Browse New, Library, Queue and Downloads from the car screen.
• Back 10 s, forward 30 s and a speed button on the player.
• Next and previous on the steering wheel skip forward and back.
• "Hey Google, play <show> on Tinypod" plays that show's latest unplayed episode.
