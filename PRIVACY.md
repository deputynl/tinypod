# Tinypod privacy policy

*Last updated: 3 October 2026*

Tinypod is a podcast app with no account, no backend and no analytics.
The developer does not collect, store or share any personal data.

## What stays on your phone

Your subscriptions, folders, queue, listening progress, history and
downloaded episodes are stored only in the app's own storage on your
device. They are never sent to the developer or anyone else. If Android's
device backup is turned on, the system may include this data in your own
backup with Google, under Google's terms; Tinypod itself does nothing
with it.

## Network requests

Tinypod only connects to:

- **The podcast feeds you subscribe to**, and the audio files and artwork
  those feeds link to. These servers belong to the podcasts' publishers
  and hosting providers, who receive the usual request details (such as
  your IP address and the app's user agent) and may count downloads, as
  they would for any podcast app.
- **Apple's iTunes Search API**, only when you search for a podcast by
  name. Your search terms are sent to Apple to find matching shows.

There are no ads, no tracking or analytics SDKs, and no crash reporting.

## Permissions

Tinypod uses internet access (to fetch feeds and audio) and a
foreground service (so playback continues in the background and in
Android Auto). It does not request access to your location, contacts,
microphone, camera or files.

## Children

Tinypod is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Contact
